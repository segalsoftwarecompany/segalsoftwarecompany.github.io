Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how SEGAL SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT LTD ("we", "us"), a company registered in the Republic of Cyprus (reg. no. HE 498150), handles information in connection with our games and this website.

Who is responsible

Information we collect

  • Game data. Progress, settings and in-game purchases needed to run the game and restore your progress.

Where a game uses third-party services (for example analytics or advertising), this is disclosed on its App Store page, and those services process data under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

To provide and improve our games, process purchases, prevent fraud, respond to support requests and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing the game), for our legitimate interests (security, bug fixing, improving the game), with your consent where required, and to comply with law.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to or restrict processing, and request data portability. Email contact@segalsoftware.com. You may also complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new date.
